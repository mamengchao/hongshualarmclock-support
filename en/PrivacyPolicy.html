Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Hongshu Alarm (hereinafter 'this app') collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Please read this privacy policy carefully before using this app. By using this app, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to all terms of this privacy policy.

1. Information We Collect

This app only collects the following necessary information:

Alarm Data The alarm name, time, ring mode, repeat rules, etc. that you set are stored only locally on your device and will not be uploaded to any server.
Device Permissions This app needs notification permission to remind you at the scheduled time. This permission is used solely for local alarm reminder functionality.
In-App Purchase Info When you purchase Premium, the App Store provides a transaction receipt for verification. It does not contain your payment card information. The developer cannot access your Apple ID password or payment credentials.

2. Data Storage

  • All alarm data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. We do not transmit any data to external servers.
  • The data required for dismissing alarms (including but not limited to poems, math problems, text, etc.) is read from the app bundle's resource files and does not require an internet connection.
  • In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. Transaction records are maintained by Apple. The developer does not store your payment information.

3. Permissions

This app may request the following permissions:

Notification Permission Used to send you reminders when an alarm triggers. You can enable or disable this permission at any time in the device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 This app's in-app purchase functionality is implemented through Apple's StoreKit framework. Transaction processing, payment verification, and receipt management are handled by Apple. Apple may collect transaction-related information. For details, please see Apple's Privacy Policy.

4.2 This app itself does not actively collect, upload, or store users' personal information. All alarm data is stored locally on the device. The app does not contain any third-party advertising SDKs and will not actively connect to the internet except for App Store communication necessary for in-app purchases.

4.3 The iOS system may automatically back up app data to iCloud. This behavior is controlled by the system and cannot be intervened by the developer. You are advised to review and manage your iCloud backup settings in the system settings.

5. Data Security

5.1 Since all alarm data is stored locally on the device, there is no risk of leakage during data transmission. We recommend that you properly safeguard your device and prevent it from being lost or used by others without authorization.

5.2 The security of in-app purchase transactions is guaranteed by the Apple App Store's payment system. The developer does not access your payment information (such as credit card numbers, etc.).

6. Children's Privacy

6.1 This app is intended for general users and is not specifically designed for children. This app will not intentionally collect personal information from minors under the age of 14.

6.2 If a guardian discovers that a minor is using this app without their consent and has provided personal information, please contact us through the contact information at the bottom of this policy. We will delete the relevant information within a reasonable time.

7. User Rights and Data Control

7.1 Since all alarm data is stored only locally on your device, you can manage it directly through the app's edit or delete functions. Uninstalling this app will automatically remove all alarm data from your device.

7.2 For in-app purchases, you can use the "Restore Purchases" feature on the About page to restore previously purchased items. For refunds, please apply through Apple's official channel at reportaproblem.apple.com.

8. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be notified in a prominent location within the app. You are advised to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or requests regarding this privacy policy (including accessing, correcting, or deleting your personal information), please contact us through the in-app feedback channel. We will respond within a reasonable time.
