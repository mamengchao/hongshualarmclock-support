❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: The alarm didn't ring / Alarm not going off

Alarm reminders depend on your device's hardware (speaker, vibration motor) and system notifications. Please check the following:

  • Ensure the app has notification and alarm permissions enabled in Settings → Apps → Hongshu Alarm.
  • Check that your device is not in Silent mode, Do Not Disturb, or Focus mode.
  • Confirm that the alarm's "On" toggle is enabled in the alarm list.
  • If you are using a power-saving mode, some system alarm schedules may be delayed.

Q: How do I dismiss an alarm?

When the alarm rings, a challenge will appear. You need to solve the math problem, complete the game, or finish the memory challenge to dismiss the alarm.

Q: What are the differences between Free and Premium?

Free version: up to 3 alarms, first 3 ringtones, arithmetic only (evaluate sub-type), easy difficulty only.

Premium version: unlimited alarms, all 20+ ringtones, all question types (arithmetic + games + memory), all difficulty levels, and smart holiday skip (where available).

Q: How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Premium is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, go to About → Premium Comparison and tap "Restore Purchase".

Q: Why does the app say "no holiday data" for my region?

Holiday data is currently available for Mainland China (CN), Taiwan (TW), and Hong Kong (HK) only. If Smart Holiday Skip is not visible in your Premium comparison, it means your region is not yet supported.

❓ 常见问题

问：闹钟没有响

闹钟提醒依赖于设备硬件（扬声器、振动马达）和系统通知机制。请检查以下事项：

  • 确保在系统"设置 → 应用 → 红薯闹钟"中已开启通知和闹钟权限。
  • 检查设备是否处于静音模式、勿扰模式或专注模式。
  • 确认闹钟列表中的开关已打开（绿色状态）。
  • 如果开启了省电模式，部分系统闹钟调度可能会被延迟。

问：如何关闭闹钟？

闹钟响铃后会出现一道挑战题，您需要答对数学题、完成游戏或记忆挑战才能关闭闹钟。

问：基础版和高级版有什么区别？

基础版：最多 3 个闹钟、前 3 首铃声、仅算术题型、仅求结果子类、仅简单难度。

高级版：闹钟数量无限制、全部 20 余种铃声、算术 + 游戏 + 记忆全部题型、所有难度、智能跳过节假日（数据支持地区）。

问：如何恢复高级版购买？

高级版为一次性购买，与 Apple ID 绑定。重新安装或更换设备后，请前往"关于 → 功能对比"页面，点击"恢复购买"即可。

问：为什么显示"该地区暂无节假日数据"？

目前仅支持中国大陆（CN）、台湾（TW）和香港（HK）的节假日数据。如果 Premium 对比页面未显示"智能跳过节假日"行，说明您所在地区暂不支持此功能。
